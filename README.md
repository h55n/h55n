### `active_projects`

**🏔️ [SlopeSense](https://github.com/h55n) — Landslide Risk Intelligence Platform**
Fuses 6 satellite/weather sources into a probabilistic Failure Probability Index (1km² resolution, refreshed every 6h). LightGBM calibrated on a physics-based LHASA v2 model, backtested 6/6 against historic landslide events. Sub-120ms p95 latency, CAP v1.2 government-standard alert feed, WhatsApp dispatch under 30s. 100+ pytest tests.

**🛰️ River Watch — Satellite Anomaly Detection**
Dual signal-fusion (Sentinel-1 SAR + Sentinel-2 NDWI) surfacing defensible anomalies for illegal sand mining across 4 Indian river hotspots. Built for human-in-the-loop review by journalists and NGOs — never claims "confirmed illegal," always requires seasonal adjustment.

**🧸 fumii — AI Companion (Hardware + Desktop App)**
Palm-sized AI companion with a tiered RAG-style memory system (core identity → episodic retrieval → rolling emotional state), multi-provider LLM abstraction (Mistral/OpenAI/Claude/Ollama), and token-by-token streaming. IEEE CodeBhoomi 2026 Top 10 national finalist.

**💬 DeTalks — Multi-Agent Mental Wellness Platform**
Dual-agent safety architecture: a triage agent ("Disha") + a silent real-time crisis monitor ("Kavach"), with 5-level severity routing and mandatory human handoff. Designed explicitly against unhealthy parasocial dependency. IEEE CodeBhoomi 2026 Top 10 national finalist.

**🎙️ Mike — AI Voice Dictation Tool**
